# Example University — Policy on Student Use of Generative AI

> **This is a fictional example** used by `config/examples/one-day-workshop.yaml` to show how a policy attachment shapes a generated course. Replace it with your institution's real policy.

## 1. Scope
Applies to all taught modules. "Generative AI" (GenAI) means tools that produce text, code, images or other content from prompts, e.g. ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Claude.

## 2. Permitted use
Unless a module handbook says otherwise, students **may** use GenAI to:
- brainstorm, plan and outline work;
- explain concepts, generate practice questions, and get feedback on drafts;
- generate code snippets, provided the student can explain every line submitted.

## 3. Prohibited use
Students **may not**:
- submit GenAI-generated content as their own without disclosure;
- use GenAI in closed-book assessments or where the assessment brief forbids it;
- upload personal data of others, confidential material, or unpublished research to GenAI tools.

## 4. Disclosure
Any submitted work that used GenAI must include a short *AI use statement*: which tool(s), for what purpose, and what the student changed. Absence of a statement asserts no GenAI was used.

## 5. Responsibility
Students remain fully responsible for the accuracy, originality and appropriateness of submitted work. "The AI said so" is not a defence.

## 6. Data protection
Only tools approved by the University (see the IT Services list) may be used with University data. Free consumer tools may be used only with non-sensitive material.
